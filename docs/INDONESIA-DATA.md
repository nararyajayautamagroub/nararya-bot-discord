## Refresh policy

The baseline Indonesia data refresh is 24 hours.

Source-specific TTLs may be shorter when required for correctness or health monitoring.

## Scraper requirement

All non-JKT48 external data sources require a registered adapter, a source contract, a parser or API client, health reporting, and tests.

## Ramadan

Ramadan dates remain estimates until official Indonesian determination is published.

Prayer times are source-dependent.

## Restaurant prices

Restaurant menu data is read-only public aggregation through the MenuKuliner.net adapter.

Displayed prices are informational and may differ by branch, delivery platform, tax, promotion, or menu changes.
